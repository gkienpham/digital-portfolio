Project 2: Silly Walks Robot

The Silly Walks challenge was to build a LEGO SPIKE Prime robot that moves forward without using wheels. The robot runs on the hub’s default program, so the whole project was about mechanical design rather than code: how to propel the robot forward without wheels, how leg length affects its motion, and how to get traction on a smooth floor.

My robot walks on legs attached directly to a motor. As the motor turns, the legs swing in a stepping motion that carries the robot forward. The video below shows it walking.
